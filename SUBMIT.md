**Logline:** On the night of the river festival, the lanterns have drifted loose. Guide them home.

**Description (paste):**
Lantern Drift is a quiet sliding puzzle on a night river, built on one rule you learn in a single move: each lantern drifts
in a straight line until a stone or another lantern stops it, and every lantern of the same colour drifts together.
Bring each one home to the ring of its colour.
Clever systems: that one rule stacks. Moving one colour moves all its lanterns, so you plan which lantern stops which,
and by the later nights the three colours act as moving walls for each other.
Clear rules: Night 1 teaches the whole game with a guide arrow and no text tutorial. The ring shows where to go, and the
move counter and par say how well you did. If you get stuck, Hint shows the next drift and Undo/Restart are one key away.
There are 28 nights. A solver checked every one, and each night's par is the proven minimum number of moves, so three
stars always means a perfect solution. Craft: canvas-drawn board with soft drift animation and a glow on arrival, a painted title illustration, and a CC0 ambient score with chimes on each homecoming. A new 'Tonight's lantern' puzzle arrives every day. It plays in any browser with keyboard, swipe or tap. The colours also have shapes
(circle, diamond, triangle), so it is colour-blind friendly. The music and sounds are CC0 recordings (credits below).
